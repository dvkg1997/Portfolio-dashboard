Portfolio Engine & Risk Manager
Streamlit-based web application designed for finance professionals to optimize portfolios, 
simulate systemic stress, and generate institutional-grade investment reports.
🚀 Core Features
1. Dynamic Universe SelectionLive Sourcing: Fetches real-time constituents for Nifty 50, Next 50, Midcap, 500, and all major Sectoral Indices (Banking, IT, Auto, etc.) directly from NSE.Randomized Sampling: Allows for "What-if" testing by sampling a subset of assets from broad indices to find the most efficient clusters.
2. Multi-Asset "Safe Haven" IntegrationIncludes bolt-on hedges: Gold (Domestic & Global) and Treasury Bonds (10Y G-Sec & US TLT).Currency Adjustment: Automatically converts US-denominated assets (Gold/USD Treasuries) into INR terms using live USD/INR exchange rates to ensure domestic return accuracy.3. Advanced Risk AnalyticsCholesky Stress Testing: Uses Cholesky Decomposition to run 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations that respect historical asset correlations.Expected Shortfall (CVaR): Calculates the average loss in the worst 1% of scenarios, providing a deeper look at "Tail Risk" than standard VaR.Systemic Stress Slider: A unique "Correlation Breakout" feature that manually blends the historical correlation matrix toward 1.0 to simulate a total market liquidity crisis.4. Mathematical OptimizersEfficient Frontier: Visualized using a sparsity-inducing Dirichlet distribution to explore the absolute edges of the risk-return space.Three Strategy Profiles:Max Sharpe: Maximum risk-adjusted efficiency.Min Volatility: The absolute lowest variance point.Aggressive: Maximum expected historical return.🛠️ Installation & Setup1.
